   └─ Risk: Silent data loss, app crashes on quota exceeded
   └─ Fix: Implement try-catch with quota checking (See Critical-Fixes.md)
   └─ Est. time: 1-2 hours

2. ❌ No Error Boundary components
   └─ Risk: Single component error crashes entire application

   └─ Est. time: 30 minutes

   └─ Risk: Regressions, untested audio engine behavior
   └─ Fix: Add unit tests for audio engines, sequencer (See recommendations)
   └─ Est. time: 3-5 days

4. ❌ Unused dependency: @google/genai
   └─ Risk: Unnecessary bundle bloat
   └─ Fix: Remove with "npm uninstall @google/genai"
   └─ Est. time: 5 minutes
