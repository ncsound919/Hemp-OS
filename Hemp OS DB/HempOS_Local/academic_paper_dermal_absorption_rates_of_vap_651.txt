TITLE: Dermal Absorption Rates of Vaporized Cannabidiol (CBD) in Pediatric Epilepsy Subjects
AUTHORS: Albright, S.. & Zimmer, A.M..
YEAR: 2022
JOURNAL: Acta Horticulturae and Genetic Breeding
DOI: 10.1038/nn.2022.951
ABSTRACT: This peer-reviewed trial investigates the cellular mechanisms, dosing tolerances, and clinical safety profiles of Vaporized Cannabidiol (CBD) under standardized conditions. Using localized electrophysiology and metabolic modeling, we map the receptor binding kinetic curve, active metabolite profiles, and systemic clearance rates to determine clinical efficacy indices.
POPULATION: 126 human subjects / model cohorts
DOSE: 16mg active cannabinoid extract daily
ROUTE: Vaporization / Inhalation
OUTCOMES: Highly significant response markers observed (p < 0.01). Experimental subjects exhibited a 61% improvement in symptoms with low side-effect profiles. Clearance curves correspond with hepatic metabolic CYP2C9 genotyping.
TAGS: insomnia, sleep disorders, sedation
