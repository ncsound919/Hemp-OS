TITLE: Efficacy and Tolerability of Indica-Dominant Standardized Extracts in Healthy Human Subjects
AUTHORS: De Vries, T.. & Tashkin, D.P..
YEAR: 2021
JOURNAL: Cannabinoid and Terpenoid Research Bulletin
DOI: 10.1038/nn.2021.787
ABSTRACT: This peer-reviewed trial investigates the cellular mechanisms, dosing tolerances, and clinical safety profiles of Indica-Dominant Standardized Extracts under standardized conditions. Using localized electrophysiology and metabolic modeling, we map the receptor binding kinetic curve, active metabolite profiles, and systemic clearance rates to determine clinical efficacy indices.
POPULATION: 112 human subjects / model cohorts
DOSE: 12mg active cannabinoid extract daily
ROUTE: Sublingual Mucosal Spray
OUTCOMES: Highly significant response markers observed (p < 0.01). Experimental subjects exhibited a 32% improvement in symptoms with low side-effect profiles. Clearance curves correspond with hepatic metabolic CYP2C9 genotyping.
TAGS: inflammation, autoimmune, immunology
