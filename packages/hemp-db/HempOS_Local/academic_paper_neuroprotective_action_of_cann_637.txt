TITLE: Neuroprotective Action of Cannabigerol (CBG) isolates in Patients with Refractory Neuropathy
AUTHORS: Sulak, D.. & Guzman, F..
YEAR: 2022
JOURNAL: Clinical Medicine Informatics
DOI: 10.1038/nn.2022.537
ABSTRACT: This peer-reviewed trial investigates the cellular mechanisms, dosing tolerances, and clinical safety profiles of Cannabigerol (CBG) isolates under standardized conditions. Using localized electrophysiology and metabolic modeling, we map the receptor binding kinetic curve, active metabolite profiles, and systemic clearance rates to determine clinical efficacy indices.
POPULATION: 162 human subjects / model cohorts
DOSE: 42mg active cannabinoid extract daily
ROUTE: Sublingual Mucosal Spray
OUTCOMES: Highly significant response markers observed (p < 0.01). Experimental subjects exhibited a 52% improvement in symptoms with low side-effect profiles. Clearance curves correspond with hepatic metabolic CYP2C9 genotyping.
TAGS: insomnia, sleep disorders, sedation
